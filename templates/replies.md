# Reply and comment templates

For three situations: commenting on **other** launches (many free tiers require 1–3 helpful comments first), commenting on **your own** listing, and asking supporters for votes. `{}` = fill in.

Ground rules:
- Open the product (or at least read its page) before commenting; name **one concrete detail**. Platforms delete "Nice tool!" and some require "thoughtful, specific, helpful" comments to unlock a free launch.
- Never claim experience you don't have ("been using it for months"). If you haven't tried it, ask or observe.
- 2–4 sentences, one idea per comment. No links to your own product in other people's threads.

## A. Comments on other launches

1. **Specific praise** — what's good and why.
   > Congrats on the launch! The {feature} is a smart touch: most tools here make you {pain}, and you skipped that step entirely.
2. **User perspective** — who you are, why you'd use it.
   > As a {role} who {does X every week}, {feature} is the part I'd use first. Saving {task} would be a real win.
3. **Genuine question** (the safest default).
   > How does {product} handle {edge case}? Does it {A} or {B}?
4. **Friendly comparison.**
   > I use {alternative} for {task}. The biggest difference I see is {difference}. Was that the reason you built it?
5. **Constructive suggestion** — praise, then one idea.
   > Clean onboarding. One idea: showing {preview/example} before sign-up would make the value obvious even faster.
6. **Bug / UX heads-up.**
   > Heads-up in case it helps: on {browser/mobile}, {element} {issue}. Otherwise the flow felt smooth.
7. **Pricing question.**
   > The free tier is generous. Is {paid plan} aimed at teams or solo makers? Trying to see where I'd land.
8. **Use-case extension.**
   > Could see this working for {other use case} too, e.g. {example}. Have users tried it that way?
9. **Founder story.**
   > What made you start building this? A problem you hit yourself at {context}?
10. **Technical curiosity.**
    > How are you handling {technical aspect} under the hood? The results look surprisingly {quality}.
11. **Roadmap.**
    > What's next on the roadmap? A {tool} integration would make this an instant install for me.
12. **Design / copy praise.**
    > The landing copy is unusually clear: "{quote}" told me in one line what it does.
13. **Tried-it feedback** (only if you did).
    > Tried it on {small task}: took {time}, and {honest impression}. {One thing to improve}.
14. **Light humour** (still specific).
    > {Pain point} has ruined my {time of day} for years. Upvoted out of pure self-interest 😄
15. **First-launch encouragement.**
    > Shipping a first launch is the hardest part, congrats! {Detail} shows real care. Good luck this week.

## B. On your own listing

16. **Maker first comment** (≤200 / ≤500 / ≤1000 versions in product.yaml).
    > Hi everyone! I built {product} because {personal pain}. It {one-line what it does}. Free to try ({free tier}). I'd love feedback on {specific question}.
17. **Thanks + follow-up question.**
    > Thanks {name}! What would you try it on first? That helps us tune {feature}.
18. **Honest answer to a limitation.**
    > Fair question. Today it {limitation}. We chose that because {reason}; {improvement} is on the list.
19. **Taking a feature request.**
    > Great idea, adding it to the list. What would the ideal version look like in your workflow?
20. **Launch-day update** (real numbers only).
    > Launch-day update: {real number or learning}. Thanks for all the questions; answering every one today.
21. **Thank-you note after an upvote** (≤280).
    > Thanks for the upvote! {Product} is free to try with {free tier}; tell me what you'd want next.

## C. Asking for votes (to your own network, never vote rings)

22. **Launch-day DM / Slack.**
    > We're live on {platform} today. If {product} looks useful, an upvote helps a lot (needs a {platform} account; Google login works): {link}
23. **X / LinkedIn post.**
    > {Product} launches on {platform} today 🚀 {one-line value}. Would love your support and feedback: {link}
24. **Weekly roundup to supporters.**
    > This week {product} is on {n} launch boards: {links}. One click each makes a difference; thank you!

## Don't

- "Great product!" / "Upvoted!" / copy-pasting the same comment across launches.
- Pretending to be a user. Asking for "upvote mine and I'll upvote yours".
- Links to your product in other people's comment threads.
